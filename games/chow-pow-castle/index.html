Chow Pow

Scooping the castle…

Hold the mouse, pull back away from where you want to shoot, and let go. 1-4 fire hero power-ups, Q switches arrows, ESC pauses. Hold your phone either way up. Press anywhere, pull back, and let go to shoot; tap a glowing hero to fire a power-up. Play as Madame Mint Chip on the tower of the ice cream castle. Ten waves of Barbaric Burgers march on the gate, ending with Beef Erickson himself. Spend coins between waves on sharper arrows and Dairy heroes for the wall. Turn your sound on!
