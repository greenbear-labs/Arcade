Dwarves vs Orcs

Forging the axes…

Best on a computer. Dwarves vs Orcs is a real-time strategy game built for a mouse and keyboard. Touch controls work, and a tablet held sideways is much nicer than a phone.

Left-click or drag to select, right-click to move, gather, build or attack (Ctrl + right-click to attack-move). WASD, the arrow keys or the screen edge scroll, the mouse wheel zooms. Command buttons have hotkeys (Q E R T Y / F G Z X V), Shift + train queues five, C opens shipments, H jumps to your hall and . finds an idle miner. Tap to select. With units selected, tap the ground to move, or tap a tree, mine, enemy, treasure or unfinished building to work on it. Drag to scroll, pinch to zoom, and touch and hold before dragging to select a group. The buttons under the top bar deselect, pick your army, find an idle miner and switch batch training. Gather food, wood and gold with your miners, build houses for more room, train axemen, bowmen and boar riders, and earn shipments of help as you gain experience. Advance through three ages, choosing a different ally each time. Orc raids grow every few minutes: ring the town bell to shelter your miners. Destroy the Orc Warcamp to win.
