Full Moon Drive-Thru

🌕🍔🐺

A werewolf is loose in the Mega Moonburger!

One player is the werewolf. Everyone else works the late shift and has to survive until closing time. Swap roles every round.

How to play
  • Move: WASD or arrow keys. On a tablet, drag anywhere on the left side.
  • Use your pickup: Space (or the USE button).
  • Survivors score a point every second they stay free, plus a bonus for lasting the round. Caught survivors become ghosts.
  • The werewolf scores for every survivor caught, and a bonus for catching everyone.
  • Vents (grey grates) are survivor-only shortcuts. Tubes and slides in the Playland work for everyone. Hide in the ball pit!
  • Swinging doors swing back after you run through. Anyone right behind you gets BONKED.
  • The drive-thru lane outside is a conveyor belt. Ride it, or fight against it.
  • Survivor pickups: 🥤 Turbo Soda (speed), 🪣 Grease Bucket (drop a slick), 🍟 Garlic Fries (shield; werewolves sneeze), 🧅 Onion Bomb (hide in the stink).
  • Werewolf pickups: 🌕 Moon Pie (howl: see everyone), 🌶️ Ghost Pepper (rage: super fast, smashes doors), 🍁 Syrup Trap (survivors get stuck), plus Turbo Soda and Grease.
  • Tasks: stand at a glowing ! station and press Space to play a quick mini game. You score points and closing time comes sooner, but the werewolf sees where you are.
  • The werewolf can sniff out the nearest survivor every so often, and gets even faster in the last 20 seconds: midnight frenzy.
