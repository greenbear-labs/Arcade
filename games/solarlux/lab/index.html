BATTLE LAB

Two prototype battle modes for playtesting. Progress here doesn't affect the main game.

Spotlight Rush

Your crew fight on their own. Tap a glowing portrait to fire her skill. Fire three skills in a row for a Showstopper.
Deck Match

Swap tiles to match three or more. Each crew member's tiles power her attacks; hearts heal. Watch the enemy's countdown.

Stage

Squad (pick 4)

Tap a crew member to add or remove her.
