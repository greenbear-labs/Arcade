Mythica Farmer

Tilling the field…

WASD walk, Shift run, Space swing, E talk, pet a hen or open doors, F use the item you picked, Q / R or the mouse wheel change tools, 1–8 pick an item, I bag, N sleep. Drag on the left to walk (push all the way to run). Tap USE, HIT and TALK on the right, slide the tool dial (top left) up or down to change tools, and tap the item bar to pick an item. Full screen always plays sideways. Talk to Grandpa Turnip for seeds, dig and water your field, catch fish with Old Gil's rod, and sell your haul to Mabel. Crops grow overnight, but only if you watered them. Feed and pet your hens for eggs, and plant for the season: every week brings summer, fall or winter. Make friends with gifts (F with an item picked) and help with requests on the quest board. Dot the Miner lends you a pickaxe for the mine: break rocks for ore and gems, find the ladder down, and watch out for slimes. Your farm saves each night when you sleep, so you can pick up where you left off.
