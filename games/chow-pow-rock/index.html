Chow Pow: Battle of the Bands

Chow Pow

Tuning the guitars…

Hit the notes as they cross the line with D F J K (or the arrow keys). Hold long notes, and press SPACE to fire Sugar Rush. Play sideways with your thumbs on the colored pads, or upright by tapping the lanes. Keep your finger down on long notes, and tap RUSH when Sugar Rush is ready. Play as Sour Bomb Sandy and beat nine rival bands, one from every Chow Pow faction, to win the Battle of the Bands. Turn your sound on!
